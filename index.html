Our Mission

No one is beyond the reach of a kind word

Chabad of Rikers brings consistent Jewish presence to one of the hardest places in New York. We don't ask what brought someone here — we ask what they need today.

Read our story →

Weekly visitation

One-on-one chaplain visits, kosher provisions, and study — every single week, holidays included.

Holiday programs

Chanukah candle-lightings, a Passover Seder, Rosh Hashanah and more — bringing the calendar inside.

Family & reentry support

We stay with people through release — connecting them to community, work, and a path forward.
