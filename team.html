From the Director

Rabbi Mendel S.

"People ask how I can go in there week after week. I tell them: when God called, Abraham didn't ask where or why. He said Hineni — Here I am. That's the whole job."

For more than twelve years, Rabbi Mendel has walked the corridors of Rikers Island carrying nothing but presence and a refusal to let anyone be forgotten. What began as a single weekly visit has grown into a full chaplaincy — and the promise that the door is never closed.
